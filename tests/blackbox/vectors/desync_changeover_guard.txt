# Desync check skips the polling -> interrupt changeover (rm32 divergence,
# refactor_log Call 45). rm32 polls BEMF on the 20 kHz tick grid, so polled
# commutations run late and the first interrupt-mode revolutions are much
# faster; AM32's |last - avg| > avg/2 read that as a desync (L431 25 %
# starts restart-looped ~50 %). The detector compares only after two full
# interrupt-mode passes following the last polling tick.
#
# Same drive as desync_detection.txt, plus ONE polling tick first so the
# main loop sees old_routine=1. Without it the desync fires at ZC 11 (that
# vector); with it the passes at ZC 6 and 12 only re-seed, and the desync
# fires at the ZC 18 pass.

# Rig tuning: asserts the fast-rotor stay-interrupt desync response
# (rm32::tuning::DesyncPolicy::HalfKickStayInterrupt), not in AM32.
@tuning rig

@config
armed=1
inputSet=1
dshot=1
EDT_ARMED=1
running=1
old_routine=1
zero_crosses=0
commutation_interval=600

@sequence
# Polling tick: no ZC, the main loop sees old_routine=1.
tick 1  | throttle=500 | old_routine=1

tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
# ZC 12: AM32's detector would fire here; the guard holds it.
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 | zero_crosses=12 old_routine=0 running=1

tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 |
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=0 |
# ZC 18: third interrupt-mode pass, the detector compares and fires
# (fast-rotor path: interrupt mode kept, still running).
tick 1  | throttle=500 zc=1 comp=1 | zero_crosses<=2 old_routine=0 running=1
